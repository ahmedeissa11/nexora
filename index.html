The house

Nexora is a quiet atelier. Each piece is designed to disappear into a room once it is understood — not to announce itself from across the table.

We finish in graphite, midnight, and oxide. Surfaces are matte, edges are quiet, and the only light we add is the light you choose.

Materials

Aluminum is milled, not stamped. Leather is vegetable-tanned. Ceramics are thrown in small lots and fired dark.

Drivers are matched in pairs. Movements are regulated by hand. Nothing in the catalog is seasonal.

Three finishes

One language, every object

Graphite

Milled aluminum, left matte. The standard temperature of the house.

Midnight

Not a void. A dark that still admits a little blue near a window.

Oxide

The warmest of the three — a brown that is almost not a colour.

Craft

Milled, matched, finished by hand.

No seasonal colours. No fourth finish because a calendar asked. The test is simple: if the object is doing its work, you stop naming it.
